Community Meals

Support

Last updated September 10, 2026

Community Meals is an app that connects volunteers with food-relief organizations that cook and deliver meals to their community. Organizations post volunteer runs; volunteers sign up to cook, pack, or drive; families and groups can claim a dish to bring instead. This page is for getting help, deleting your account, and questions from a parent or guardian — for how the app handles your information, see the privacy policy.

Get help

Questions about the app, a problem you've run into, or anything that isn't covered on this page: email irehan29@icloud.com. A real person reads every message and will reply.

Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from inside the app — you don't need to email anyone first:

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Scroll to the Danger zone section.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Re-type your account's email address to confirm.

This deletes your account. The privacy policy lists exactly what is removed, and what is kept with your name taken off it. If you'd rather have us do it for you, email irehan29@icloud.com from the address on the account and we'll take care of it.

For parents and guardians

Volunteers between 13 and 17 can join with a parent or guardian's consent, given step by step during sign-up and recorded item by item — not as one bundled checkbox. A guardian can ask at any time to see what they agreed to, withdraw any single item, withdraw consent altogether, or have the young volunteer's account deleted, by emailing irehan29@icloud.com.

There is also a page written specifically for parents and guardians, inside the app, For parents and guardians, which can be opened without signing in.

Privacy policy

For what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted, see the Community Meals privacy policy.
